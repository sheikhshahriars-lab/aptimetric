Key architecture:
  lib/irt/itemParams.ts  Single source of truth for the 12-point difficulty scale
                     and its theta mapping (difficultyToB / thetaToDifficulty).
  lib/irt/scoring.ts 3PL IRT EAP scoring, IQ (mean 100, SD 15), CI, percentile,
                      SEM, domain thetas, iqClassification, plus per-subtest scaled
                      scores (WAIS-style: mean 10, SD 3). Client sends raw answers;
                      /api/results recomputes server-side (never trusts the client).
  lib/cat/*          domain sequencing, ability-targeted difficulty adaptation
                     (QUESTIONS_PER_DOMAIN=10), timed-subtest limits, question
                     fetching (verbal from Supabase `questions` table with sourceId
                     dedup, visual via local generators + ShapeSVG).
  app/api/results    POST route: validates answers (difficulty 1-12, 60 max),
                     enforces 90-day cooldown (423), derives age_band, server-side
                     EAP scoring, stores subtest breakdown + age_band, proctor
                     flagging (>=3 suspicious).
  proxy.ts           protects /dashboard /test /results /certificate /admin /recruiter;
                     ?next= redirect after login; admin/recruiter role checks via app_role RPC.
  lib/hooks/useProctor.ts  tab-switch, blur, fast-answer (<700ms), long-pause tracking.

Monetization (both built):
  Consumer: free test, results preview + paywall (NEXT_PUBLIC_UPGRADE_URL) for full
            report + certificate, retake cooldown 90 days.
  B2B:      recruiter accounts create orgs + email invites; candidates take the test
            via invite link; score auto-links to the invitation.
